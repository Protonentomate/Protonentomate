Free, no ads, no account, no tracking. The app explains the subjects of the Swiss theory test with its own practice questions, for cars and motorcycles (B, B1, A1, A), mopeds (M), tractors (G, G40, F) and lorries (C1, C). Every answer comes with an explanation and the article of the law in its official wording (in German, French or Italian; there is no official English version). In English, German, French and Italian, for iPhone and iPad.

  • Our own practice questions on all topics of the test, not the licensed exam questions
  • Four areas: car & motorcycle, moped, tractor with trailers and implements, lorry with the additional theory test
  • Drawn traffic situations and a right-of-way game
  • “Learn today” with spaced repetition, mistakes and saved questions
  • Mock exam in the format of the theory test: basic theory until the end of 2026 with 50, from 2027 with 55 questions; moped 30, tractor 40, lorry C 40 and C1 30 questions
  • All road signs, theory, getting your licence, stopping distance calculator
  • Not offered by an authority or asa; all information without guarantee
